About

I've spent the better part of a decade making brands look like they know exactly who they are — because most of the time, that certainty is the hardest part for a founder to reach alone.

My process leans research-first: before a single mark gets sketched, I want to understand the market a brand is entering, who it's competing against for attention, and what it needs to signal to be trusted on sight.

The output is always a system, not a logo — a set of rules a team can apply for years without it falling apart.
